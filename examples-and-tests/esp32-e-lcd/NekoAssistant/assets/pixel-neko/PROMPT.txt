Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production sprite atlas for an original virtual pet running on a tiny 1-bit reflective LCD.
Create one beautiful, carefully drawn original kitten character inspired by the charm and finish of a vintage virtual pet / Tamagotchi, but not any existing copyrighted character. Cute, stylish, mischievous, very clearly a CAT, big expressive glossy eyes, short fluffy cheek tufts, triangular ears, a rounded compact body, little paws and a curved expressive tail. Subtle little collar/tag allowed. Three-quarter view looking slightly to the RIGHT so a speech balloon can point to the mouth on the right side of the head. Strong designed silhouette, appealing proportions, polished pixel art.
CRITICAL MEDIUM: Pure BLACK and WHITE only. NO gray pixels, NO colors. Convey midtones and volume with hand placed ordered/checkerboard dithering and sparse black stipple clusters. Crisp connected black outlines, clean white eye highlights, white background. NOT ASCII, NOT a typographic cat, not vector-looking clip art.
COMPOSITION: One wide sprite sheet, EXACTLY 4 columns and 2 rows, EIGHT equally sized rectangular cells, all cells plain white with NO grid lines, NO labels, NO letters, NO borders or drop shadows. Keep each entire kitten inside a generous central square area of its cell, same character size and consistent same baseline/center across all eight cells; enough white spacing to separate frames cleanly. Prefer a 1536x1024 landscape canvas. Each kitten must be fully visible including tail and paws.
Frame order left to right top row then bottom row:
0: idle eyes open, tail curled toward left.
1: gentle inhale, tail moves slightly toward center, eyes open.
2: gentle exhale, tail toward right, eyes open.
3: idle with eyes half closed in a blink.
4: eyes fully closed in a relaxed blink.
5: talking, mouth clearly a small open oval, eyes bright.
6: delighted, happy curved eyes and one paw raised for a little wave.
7: alert and curious, large round eyes, ears perked.
Keep the identity, face, torso and dither markings extremely consistent between cells. Motions should be small, legible animation keyframes rather than eight different cats. Pixel grid aesthetic with a clean silhouette that will still read when each character is displayed about 128 pixels tall. Do not include any UI or speech bubble in the atlas.
